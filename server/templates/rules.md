# Rules & privacy

## Rules

- No harassment, hate, threats, or personal attacks.
- All sketches must be Safe For Work. No sexual content or gore.
- No spam, no advertising.
- Nothing illegal.
- Credit other people's work, and respect others' intellectual property.
- Don't attempt to exploit the site in any way, including making multiple accounts to circumvent moderation decisions.
- You must be at least 13 years old to make an account.
- **Warn about flashing**. Rapid flashing or strobing can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. If your sketch flashes, say so at the start of its description.

If you see something that breaks these rules, hit the **Report** button on the sketch or comment, and a moderator will review the report as soon as possible. If you have a question about the rules, or you believe a moderation decision was made incorrectly, feel free to reach out at [contact@easl-lang.org](mailto:contact@easl-lang.org).

## Privacy

### Sharing and forking

A private sketch is visible only to you (and moderators), but once you make a sketch public, anyone can see it and run it, read its code, and fork it to make their own version. You can also make sketches unlisted such that they'll be visible to and forkable by anyone with a direct link, but they won't show up in the explore page.

### Browsing without an account

You can browse sketches and use the editor without signing up. The site counts page views by country, but doesn't store your IP address or any other personal information. No cookies are stored until you sign in. The server briefly holds your IP address in memory to enforce rate limits and count sketch views, never writes it down, and forgets it within about two hours.

When not logged into an account, anything you create with the editor will be stored locally in your browser. You can later create an account to save those local drafts as sketches.

### Your account

When you make an account, the site stores:

- your username, email address, and password (stored only as a salted hash, never as the password itself)
- the display name and bio you choose, if any
- the sketches you save, with their past versions and thumbnails
- your comments, likes, and playlists
- any reports you make

Your username, display name, bio, avatar (generated from your username), public sketches, public playlists, comments, and likes are public. Your email address is never shown to other users; only the site's moderators can see it, so they can follow up on reports.

Your email address is used only to verify your account, reset your password, and confirm a change of address. We don't send newsletters or marketing.

### Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. That immediately deletes your account and everything it owns: your sketches (with their past versions and thumbnails), comments, likes, and playlists.

### Questions and requests

To ask about your data, get a copy of it, or have something corrected or removed, write to [contact@easl-lang.org](mailto:contact@easl-lang.org).

### Data usage notes

- The site is hosted by [Hetzner](https://www.hetzner.com) in Germany. Emails are sent through [Resend](https://resend.com).
- Account data is kept until you delete it. Deleted data stays in database backups for up to 14 days, and in the hosting provider's backups for up to 7.
- After you delete your account, forks other people made of your public/unlisted sketches stay on the site. Reports you filed stay without your name, and moderation records keep your username and excerpts of moderated content.
- The site is provided as is, with no guarantee that it stays available or that your data is never lost.
